**Create (Global) Martech Limited t/a “CreateTOTALLY”**  
Company number 12928895. Statement for the financial year ended 31 December 2025.

## 1. Our commitment

CreateTOTALLY is committed to conducting business responsibly, ethically and with respect for human rights.

We have zero tolerance for modern slavery, human trafficking, forced labour, bonded labour, child labour or any other form of exploitation.

We expect the same standards from our employees, contractors, suppliers and business partners and take reasonable and proportionate steps to identify and address modern slavery risks within our business and supply chain.

CreateTOTALLY's turnover is below the £36 million threshold in section 54 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015. We publish this statement voluntarily. It sets out the steps we took during the financial year ended 31 December 2025.

## 2. Our business and supply chain

CreateTOTALLY is a SaaS technology business providing a platform that enables marketing teams to generate, organise and collaborate on creative assets in real time.

Our business is primarily technology and knowledge based. Our key suppliers and third parties include technology and cloud service providers, software providers, professional service providers, contractors, IT and equipment suppliers, and other business service providers.

We employ 8 people, based in the UK. We engage 3 contractors, based in the UK and Poland.

We recognise that modern slavery risks can arise within global technology and service supply chains, including through the manufacture of hardware, outsourced services and third-party labour.

## 3. Policies and standards

Our commitment to preventing modern slavery is supported by our wider approach to responsible business, including our:

- Code of Conduct;
- Employee and people policies;
- Whistleblowing arrangements;
- Environmental and sustainability policies; and
- Supplier and procurement practices.

Together, these establish expectations around ethical conduct, fair treatment, legal compliance and responsible business behaviour.

## 4. Risk assessment and due diligence

We take a proportionate, risk-based approach to assessing modern slavery risks.

When establishing or reviewing significant supplier and business relationships, we consider factors such as:

- The nature of the products or services being supplied;
- The location and operating environment of the supplier;
- The potential use of subcontractors or third-party labour;
- The sector in which the supplier operates;
- The supplier's own policies and standards relating to human rights and labour practices; and
- Any relevant concerns or adverse information identified through our normal business due diligence.

Where we identify a concern, we will ask the supplier to correct it, carry out further due diligence or end the relationship.

## 5. Our people and recruitment

CreateTOTALLY is committed to providing a working environment in which people are treated fairly, respectfully and with dignity.

We do not tolerate forced, compulsory or involuntary labour, human trafficking, exploitation or abusive employment practices.

We comply with applicable employment legislation and seek to ensure that employees and contractors understand the terms and conditions of their engagement.

We carry out right-to-work checks on all employees. All employees are paid above the National Living Wage.

Individuals working with CreateTOTALLY must not be subject to coercion or intimidation. Everyone who works with us is free to leave on giving contractual notice.

We use appropriate recruitment and employment practices and expect any recruitment agencies or other third parties acting on our behalf to operate lawfully and ethically.

We do not charge recruitment fees to workers and do not allow agencies acting for us to do so. We do not use practices that could place individuals in a position of debt or dependency.

## 6. Raising concerns

We encourage employees, contractors, suppliers and other business partners to raise concerns if they become aware of suspected modern slavery, human trafficking or other unethical employment practices connected with CreateTOTALLY or its supply chain.

Concerns may be raised directly with Simon Croxford, CFO at simon@createtotally.com or through the Company's whistleblowing arrangements.

Concerns can also be reported in confidence to the Modern Slavery & Exploitation Helpline on 08000 121 700.

All genuine concerns will be taken seriously and investigated appropriately.

We will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern they reasonably believe to be true.

No concerns were raised during the financial year.

## 7. Training and awareness

Relevant employees and those involved in supplier management are expected to understand CreateTOTALLY's commitment to preventing modern slavery and the importance of raising concerns where appropriate.

Where a role or business relationship presents a particular risk, additional guidance or training may be provided.

This statement is shared with all staff when they join and each time it is updated.

## 8. Governance, approval and review

Responsibility for implementing this statement sits with Simon Croxford, CFO.

They are responsible for:

- Maintaining appropriate policies and standards;
- Considering modern slavery risks where relevant to business decisions;
- Applying proportionate due diligence to relevant suppliers and business relationships;
- Ensuring concerns are appropriately addressed; and
- Reviewing this statement and our approach annually.

It will be reviewed at least annually and updated where appropriate to reflect changes in our business, supply chain or applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
